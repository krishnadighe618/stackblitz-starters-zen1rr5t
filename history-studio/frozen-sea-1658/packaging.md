## 3 thumbnail ideas (2–3 words each)

1. **"ON FOOT?!"** A long column of Swedish soldiers and horses on white sea
   ice, cracks under them, a dark Danish coast far behind. Big madder-red
   letters on the linen sky.
2. **"DENMARK LOST THIS"** The tapestry map with Scania glowing yellow and
   the rest of Denmark red, a stitched arrow pointing at it.
3. **"THE SEA FROZE"** One rider at the edge of a hole in the ice, black
   water, everything else white. Highest contrast of the three.
