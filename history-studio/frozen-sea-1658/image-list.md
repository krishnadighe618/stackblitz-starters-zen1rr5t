# Image checklist: The Army That Walked Across the Sea

38 images for Gemini or ChatGPT, in story order. The maps are not on this
list: Claude draws those in the same tapestry style.

## How to make them

1. Open a new chat in Gemini or ChatGPT and paste the **style block** below.
2. Then ask for 4 or 5 images at a time, pasting each "Scene" line.
3. Keep only your best. If one has words or letters in it, ask again.
4. Save each one with its file name, for example `01-army-on-the-ice.png`.
5. Send them to Claude in batches. Claude puts them in `images/`.

Start every new chat with the style block, so the images keep matching.

### Style block (paste first)

> Make wide 16:9 images in this style: a hand-embroidered tapestry. Wool
> thread stitched on natural, undyed linen, with visible stitches and linen
> texture. Flat colours only: madder red, woad blue, weld yellow, olive green
> and natural linen, with dark brown stitched outlines. Simple stylised
> figures, mostly in profile, like an old chronicle tapestry, but richly
> detailed. Keep the main action in the middle and leave plain linen at the
> very top and bottom. No text, no letters, no numbers, no borders, no frames.
